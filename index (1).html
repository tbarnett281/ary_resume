05 / The strategy lab

            

Good analysis starts with a better question.

            

              A dataset does not arrive with a story already inside it. Someone has to decide which variables matter, what can reasonably be compared, which assumptions are defensible, where uncertainty remains, and what the evidence means for the decision at hand.             

            

              The objective is not to use the most complicated method. It is to select a method that fits the question, understand its limitations, and communicate the result in a way that is useful to the audience making the decision.             

                           Read more thinking ↗                        
          
            

Working principle

            
              “The method should follow the question—not the other way around.”
